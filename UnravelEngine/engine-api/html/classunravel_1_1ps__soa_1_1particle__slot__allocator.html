Detailed Description

Hands out an emitter's particle slots, always the lowest free one.

Keeping the live slots packed at the bottom keeps the high-water mark - the range the GPU walks every frame - close to the live count, and means a free slot below the mark never stays free for longer than the oldest particle above it lives.

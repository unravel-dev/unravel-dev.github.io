Detailed Description

The GI object grid of one camera: per cell of 2 x 2 x 2 voxels of the camera's global distance field clipmap, the four GI instances whose surfaces are nearest within reach (cs_gi_object_grid.sc), through whose cards every global-SDF hit sampler shades a hit (gi_surface_cache.sh).

The grid follows its camera's clipmap levels, so each camera keeps its own while the cards and the atlases are shared (gi_surface_cache). A level is rebuilt when its clipmap level recomposed: in its boxes after a partial recompose, in full otherwise, two full levels per frame at most - every level at once when the instance order moved, since the cells hold instance indices.

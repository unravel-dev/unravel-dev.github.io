Detailed Description

Closest-hit ray caster over a triangle soup, on the default raytracing::backend.

Triangles are two-sided for intersection; facing is decided by the caller from get_normal, the outward normal of the triangle's winding (its geometric normal). An enclosed-volume orientation fix would be wrong here: an open sheet has no volume, and its sum only says which side of the origin the sheet lies on.

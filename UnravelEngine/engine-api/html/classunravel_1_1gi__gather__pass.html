Detailed Description

The screen probe gather (software ray tracing through the screen and the global distance field): stateless uniform screen probes, plus adaptive probes where they cannot interpolate, traced every frame, composited with an absolute per-ray clamp, filtered in probe space, projected to SH3, integrated per pixel and accumulated by a per-pixel temporal (10 frames at the default update speed).

The view's indirect diffuse: rgb = E / pi in the view's pre-exposed space, alpha = served. Rays hand the far field to the radiance cache (gi_radiance_cache) past a near field the probes trace themselves, and global distance field hits read the surface cache (gi_surface_cache_pass). The final gather quality (gi_settings::diffuse_settings::quality) sets the rays per probe, the probe spacing and the full-resolution jitter (gi_pass_common.h). The constants are in engine/rendering/gi/gi_pass_constants.h; the plan and the measurements in tasks/gi_transform.

Returns

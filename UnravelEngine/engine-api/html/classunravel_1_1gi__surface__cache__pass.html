Detailed Description

One camera's handle on the GI surface cache: the process's shared gi_surface_cache (cards, atlases, lighting) and the camera's own object grid (gi_object_grid), which follows the camera's global distance field.

Per frame, for a camera running the GI: update() records the camera as a viewer and claims the frame's update of the shared cache; the camera that wins it rasterizes the captures (the deferred pipeline) and copies them; every camera brings its object grid up to date (update_object_grid) and lights the pages nearest to it (light) before its samplers read the cache. release_targets() leaves the cache (the last camera to leave frees its atlases) and frees the object grid.

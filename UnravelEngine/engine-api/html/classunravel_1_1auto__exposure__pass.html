Detailed Description

Histogram auto exposure with temporal eye adaptation and local exposure.

A compute pass meters a luminance histogram of the post-TAA HDR image, a second one trims it to a percentile band, takes the log-average and adapts the exposure toward 0.18 * 2^bias / average (linear in stops, exponential near the target), where the bias is the compensation plus the compensation curve at the metered EV100. The result is a 1x1 RGBA32F texture, AUTO_EXPOSURE: r = adapted exposure, g = target exposure, b = applied bias in stops, a = average local exposure.

The adapted exposure never reaches the CPU: update_gpu_pre_exposure turns the last frame's into the GPU's share of the pipeline's pre-exposure (pre_exposure.h).

void unravel::auto_exposure_pass::update_gpu_pre_exposure ( gfx::render_view & rview,
const gfx::texture::ptr & environment_sh,
float manual_exposure,
bool is_auto_exposure_active )

Writes this frame's GPU share of the pre-exposure for rview into texel pre_exposure_state::gpu_texel of the view's environment SH environment_sh (pre_exposure.sh, cs_pre_exposure.sc), before any pass of the frame writes or reads scene lighting.

The share follows the adapted exposure the last frame left in AUTO_EXPOSURE while is_auto_exposure_active, and is 1 otherwise; manual_exposure is the CPU's share, which the whole pre-exposure is clamped with. Writes nothing until the program has loaded.
